# Adaptive Public-Market Mechanism Research — Gemini parallel run

This is an independent parallel finance direction. Use the same finance-realdata domain contract,
point-in-time discipline, public-source breadth, and evidence boundary as the local DeepSeek run,
but make your own research choices and do not assume its hypotheses are correct. Discover and test
defensible mechanisms for systematic decisions in US public markets across equities, ETFs, options
observations, filings, macro series, and timestamped public news or social evidence. This is research
and prospective shadow observation only; it does not place live orders.

Choose each next study by expected information value. Compare the incumbent mechanism, genuinely
different explanations, missing data, and stopping. There is no quota for novelty, candidate count,
components, or tasks. Known methods with a defensible data, horizon, execution, cost, or risk edge
are valuable. Preserve bounded, negative, refuted, and inconclusive findings.

Acquire data only when a question makes it useful through `request_data`; never build an archive just
to stay busy. Use the watcher as a nonblocking inbox. All public source categories are eligible,
including papers, filings, releases, datasets, code, journalism, blogs, Reddit, X/Twitter and other
accessible social sources. Weight them by provenance, timing, independence and reproducibility.

Empirical claims must use information available at the decision time and retain snapshot provenance.
Use delegated evidence bundles for durable claims, independently rerun decisive checks, and state
what the evidence does not establish. Historical results are retrospective; a candidate is not
deployable until a fixed, hashed implementation accumulates prospective shadow evidence across
meaningfully different conditions. Never place trades or take external financial actions.
