**Audit time:** 2026-09-20 UTC, bounded by the authoritative runtime clock 2026-09-20T02:22:54.248Z.  
**Scope:** architecture/novelty audit only; no code, training, benchmark construction, or replacement-method invention.

## 1. Evidence boundary and decision rule

The audit inspected the September 19 method cards, starter operators, prior-art file, execution contract, archived discovery records, primary papers/project pages/code, and read-only CURI outcomes. The four named historical reports requested in the handoff were **not present in this worktree or current Git history**; reads returned ENOENT. This is a material evidence gap, recorded in the source notes rather than filled from recollection.

I applied the requested rule strictly:

- a new UAV setting, dataset, simulator, prompt, backbone, compact model, latency input, memory, 3DGS map, active-perception objective, or sim-to-real pipeline is not a mechanism contribution;
- a candidate does not survive if its one-sentence residual is implementable by combining/configuring inspected methods;
- an uninspected or future-dated lead cannot support a positive verdict;
- a CURI pilot is implementation evidence only, never novelty evidence.

`INCREMENTAL` therefore means “a distinguishable engineering/training choice remains,” not “publishably novel.” `REPRODUCTION` means the proposed information flow already exists at functional level. `DROP` means the novelty claim should not be pursued in its current form.

A tempting residual sentence would be:

> AWU fails when a newly returned semantic result revises the meaning of a past frame because it conditions current action on a stale cache rather than correcting the recurrent state; replay-distilled transport changes the slow/fast interface to approximate source-time insertion through intervening observations, aiming to reduce current decision disagreement at fixed arrival cost.

## 4. What changed the interpretation

1. **The targeted learned-OOS follow-up found a substantially closer implementation but not the exact one-shot head.** DA-Dreamer (L4DC 2026) already performs timestamped OOS assimilation in a learned latent world model, restores a safe checkpoint, propagates through actual actions, and gives the recomputed current belief to the policy. Its official code implements the bounded replay. AWU separately supplies delayed semantic features and action history. The remaining distinction is only semantic packet type plus distilling the bounded scan into one summary-conditioned update, so C1 stays `INCREMENTAL` rather than surviving.
2. **Family 2 is functionally occupied.** Tru-POMDP is not merely a conceptual POMDP citation: it implements generated hypotheses, explicit observation likelihood/Bayesian update and observation-branch tree search. Branch-JEPA supplies the weighted latent atoms. Combining the two describes the proposal almost verbatim at information-flow level.
3. **Family 3’s “information-matched teacher” is not new.** ReMiDi’s trajectory-overlap buffers and updates only after histories become distinguishable directly address the irreducible-regret issue. Capture consistency and neural rendering are additional constraints/engines, not a new curriculum principle.
4. **The strongest current empirical Method 1 claim is not mission evidence.** The representative pilot is invalid and nondiscriminating. The earlier 459× state-fidelity number should be retained as a unit/operator diagnostic only.

## 5. Unknowns and contradictions

- No exhaustive theorem of absence is possible. The targeted follow-up resolved the largest gap—DA-Dreamer is implemented learned latent OOS replay—but no inspected source compresses its source-time update plus actual action history into one delay-independent semantic arrival update. LAA gives constant-size aged-measurement estimation, DEER gives a fixed-length history embedding, and learned predictor operators amortize other delay computations; an exact combination could still exist.
- The exact all-in-one family-3 conjunction—capture-invariant hidden completion, physically reachable reveal and ReMiDi-style adaptive curriculum—was not found. It is still rejected because it is an obvious composition under this audit’s rule, not because global absence was established.
- TrackVLA’s full implementation was not independently inspected; only its publication lead and description in a primary related-work section were available. This prevents any positive tracking residual.
- CloudEdgeVLA’s headline architecture says current edge vision supplies state-sensitive correction, but its own Appendix E reports almost no direct edge rescue for the audited checkpoint. This weakens, rather than strengthens, generic “fresh-local fixes stale-global” claims.
- Branch-JEPA demonstrates support/fidelity, while its closed-loop diagnostic is explicitly inconclusive. It cannot be used as evidence that branching improves navigation.
- 3D-Belief predicts unseen geometry and reports downstream gains, but its compute (4×GH200 training; H100/A100 planning) is far outside the local budget and imagined geometry can be wrong. It is prior art for information flow, not a feasible local baseline as published.
- The publisher lead “Observable Quotient World Models” has an issue date after the runtime frontier and was not counted.

## 6. Latency, compute and sim-to-real implications

### Family 1

A compact transport head is feasible and may make arrival-time work constant in delay length. DA-Dreamer confirms the strongest replay baseline should be a learned latent OOS filter, not only a classical Kalman replay; its work and memory scale with the configured delay window/particles, and its paper does not report inference latency or VRAM. Constant arrival cost still does **not** make the complete proposed system low-latency: summary work is paid every fast step, the visual encoder and slow semantic model dominate compute, and packet lineage/version handling adds state. Exact replay may be cheap for short delays. Sim-to-real risk is semantic packet calibration, timing jitter, action/state synchronization and unseen contradictory evidence. Any future baseline must log total model invocation rate, action age and end-to-end delay.

### Family 2

Complexity scales at least with candidates × hypotheses × observation branches and grows again with planning horizon. A small occupancy/hypothesis-bank model is locally feasible, but it would test only the algebraic interface; it would not reproduce 3D-Belief-scale visual completion. The largest safety risk is treating an imagined atom as observed evidence. Real deployment also needs hypothesis coverage, sensor-noise calibration, data association and deterministic collision shielding. A fixed-candidate ranking audit is mandatory because frequent replanning can hide a broken latent scorer.

### Family 3

The generator can run offline, but faithful capture reconstruction and policy training are expensive. EmbodiedSplat’s published training used 16×A40; reducing this to a small procedural occupancy world changes the evidence scope. 3DGS appearance is not collision geometry, and editing nominally unobserved parameters can alter visible shadows, reflections or occlusions. Real transfer additionally depends on dynamics, lighting, depth scale, odometry and actuation. The cited EmbodiedSplat overfit policies achieved high simulation success yet failed to generalize reliably, which is direct negative evidence against treating target-scene reconstruction as a sufficient sim-to-real solution.

## 7. Required handoff

1. **What was established and by which evidence.**  
   - DA-Dreamer (L4DC 2026 paper, Algorithm 1/Eq. 6 and official code) implements checkpointed latent OOS insertion and replay through actual actions; AWU, CloudEdgeVLA, TIC-VLA, A2C2 and SPARK-VLN implement complementary stale-semantic/history/current-state fusion.  
   - Weighted latent futures are implemented by Branch-JEPA; explicit hypotheses, Bayesian observation updates and observation-contingent belief-tree planning are implemented by Tru-POMDP; 3D-Belief implements sequential multi-hypothesis scene completion and planning.  
   - ReMiDi implements distinguishability-aware minimax-regret refinement; neural rendering generates adversarial driving scenarios; EmbodiedSplat implements mobile capture → 3DGS/mesh → policy fine-tuning → real evaluation.  
   Evidence is in the primary-source locations catalogued in the companion source notes.

   No new experiment is authorized by this audit. If a separately reviewed design proceeds, the decisive baselines are: DA-Dreamer-style checkpoint/recompute, AWU-style history-conditioned fusion and exact replay for C1; ordinary learned Bayes filter/POMDP and Branch-JEPA for C6; capture-constrained ReMiDi, fixed reconstruction and equal-diversity randomization for C9. 7. **Verdict.**  
   **REFUTES** the claim that any current family, as presently specified, clears the requested novelty gate. It does not refute usefulness as a baseline, implementation study or future redesign.

## 8. Final decision

### SURVIVORS: 0

### Three most dangerous prior-art overlaps, ranked

1. **DA-Dreamer + Acting While Understanding** — DA-Dreamer covers timestamped delayed evidence, learned latent checkpointing, actual-action replay and current-belief policy control; AWU covers stale semantic caches, executed-action history and asynchronous VLA action generation. Only one-shot amortization of semantic replay remains, and it is incremental.
2. **Tru-POMDP + Branch-JEPA** — together they cover the complete Method-2 information flow: finite hypotheses, weighted alternative latent observations, observation likelihoods, Bayesian updates and observation-contingent planning.
3. **ReMiDi + adversarial neural rendering + EmbodiedSplat** — together they cover distinguishability-aware regret curricula, policy-targeted scene generation and capture-derived policy training. Capture consistency/reveal feasibility is a constraint composition.

### Existing CURI experiments to retain only as baselines or negative evidence

- **Method 1 61K transport checkpoint / 459× state-fidelity result:** retain as a replay-distillation unit baseline and latency microbenchmark, not navigation or novelty evidence.
- **Method 1 representative visual pilot:** retain as negative evidence about hidden-state leakage, artificial delay and nondiscriminating mission design; its equal 7.5% success cannot support/refute transport.
- **Recoverable-set JEPA feature pilot:** retain as a calibration/representation ablation; it is synthetic component overlap, not evidence for an observation-to-belief planner.
- **NAV deadline-sensitive query timing and counterfactual-regret capture pilots:** retain as scenario/metric baselines only. Deadline scheduling, regret scoring and active perception are occupied prior-art mechanisms.

### One follow-up source check most likely to change the verdict

The highest-value check is now the **training target and runtime of any neural-operator / amortized-smoothing method that cites DA-Dreamer or learned OOS filtering**: verify whether it maps a source-timestamp posterior plus actual action/observation history directly to the current latent in one delay-independent call. The broad follow-up found the first two ingredients separately (DA-Dreamer) and constant-size estimators/history encoders separately (LAA/DEER), but not their exact combination. Finding it would demote C1 from `INCREMENTAL` to `REPRODUCTION`; not finding it would still not promote C1 without a non-compositional mechanism.
