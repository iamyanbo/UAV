The structural gap

3D Gaussian Splatting: mature simulator, absent world model

This is the finding that matters most for a SLAM/3DGS background — the geometric and generative threads of this literature have not merged.

✓ 3DGS as differentiable simulator — mature

  • Splat-Nav — safe corridors + pose replanning, 126 real flights
  • SOUS VIDE / FiGS — policy trained purely in a 130fps GS sim, zero-shot to 105 real flights
  • GRaD-Nav — gradients flow through renderer + dynamics
  • ActiveGS, VISTA — active reconstruction, 6x success on real quadrotor

✗ 3DGS as generative world model / VLM input — absent in air

  • GST-VLA, ManiGaussian — Gaussians tokenized directly into a policy — manipulation only
  • GWM, GAF, GEM — "Gaussian world models" predicting future Gaussian states — manipulation / driving only
  • WorldFly / WorldVLN / ImagineUAV world models run on raw pixels or video latents — never explicit Gaussians
  • Zero aerial instances of either pattern found

Where to build

Four research bets, ranked

Each idea was checked against prior art. Verdicts: genuinely open, open-but-risky, or already crowded.

1
JEPA over compressed Gaussians
highest noveltytwo passes confirm gap

Predict future latent Gaussian scene state instead of pixels or raw video. Inherits SkyJEPA's control architecture + a mature Gaussian-compression toolkit (LightGaussian, 15–100×). No paper occupies this intersection.

2
Language-conditioned JEPA for UAVs
genuinely open

Language+JEPA exists (ground robots). JEPA+control exists (SkyJEPA, drones). Language+world-model exists (WorldFly, but generative). The three-way combo doesn't — yet.

3
Gaussian-native policies for UAVs
open, aerial-specific

GST-VLA and ManiGaussian tokenize Gaussians directly into a policy — thriving in manipulation. Zero instances in free-flight navigation. De-risked by mature compression research.

4
Matryoshka VLM/VLA unification
open but risky

Nested-weight training has never touched an action head. But co-training evidence is grim: OpenVLA scores near-zero on VQA benchmarks after action fine-tuning, even with dedicated anti-forgetting engineering.

×
JEV (TypeSafe AI)
not viable foundation

One day old at time of research, no architecture paper, cloud/API-only (defeats "onboard" framing), outputs limited to discrete choices/scalars — not continuous flight control. The one existing drone demo is a single-run, author-disclaimed hobbyist project in simulation only. Useful as a citation showing the trend, not as infrastructure.

Strongest single project
JEPA-style latent prediction
+
Compressed / tokenized 3DGS scene state
=
A language-conditioned world model that imagines future geometry — not future pixels — for real-time UAV navigation

Two independent research passes flagged this exact intersection from opposite directions. It's the one direction that specifically rewards a SLAM/3DGS background over a language-model-only or robotics-only one.
