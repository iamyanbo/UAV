# Research lifecycle

The current design is one orchestrator, one delegated agent at a time, and one watcher. The delegated slot also performs independent criticism with fresh context. The orchestrator interprets the handoff before another delegation. The tool surface does not expose recursive agent spawning, and the database enforces sequential delegation and a single executor/critic run.

Run `npm run build` before starting services or deploying source changes. Daemons use `dist/cli.js` and compiled extensions; they do not depend on runtime TypeScript transpilation.

Scientific reasoning lives in ordinary prose and files. There are no generated coverage categories, mandatory hypothesis frames, research deadlines or experiment quotas. The lead chooses useful questions and methods. A case may remain exploratory; closing it does not certify that the infobase is sufficient.

In finance, investigation and executable experiments form one learning loop. A delegated task can investigate, implement and evaluate an idea without a prior supported outcome or accepted synthesis. The lead weighs further evidence gathering against a concrete experiment and follows a candidate worth observing through review and paper enrollment. The candidate wake summary exposes outstanding enrollment requirements from the same ledger checks used for activation, including the latest independent review. Selection is labelled separately from actual broker execution. These facts guide agent judgment; they do not create a strategy quota or force every finding into a trade. See [the finance mission](quant-mission.md).

Public discovery uses `request_discovery` with an explicit URL and a freeform rationale. The watcher collects or follows permitted feeds, APIs and documents, archives original responses and changed versions, and exposes access failures. It does not run another research agent or automatically promote source content to trading features. See [free discovery](free-discovery.md) for endpoints, provenance, access rules and remaining quant information gaps.

`plan_investigation` routes a chosen case using explicit identifier/state arguments. A wait can depend on evidence alone or include an agent-selected review date. In continuous mode, the operator controls direction-wide suspension and the lead has no `pause_research` tool. Market closure or an immature paper sample delays that investigation; the lead pursues other consequential research. Outside continuous mode, `pause_research` persists its observation boundary so evidence arriving during the lead turn, or across a restart, wakes the lead. Unchanged waits do not cause periodic paid reconsideration. Forecast maturity prompts observation without forcing a score when evidence is unavailable.

Continuous mode also owns the empty-slot boundary. After a successful lead turn leaves no assigned work, the runtime delegates the standing mission and current agenda to the existing researcher, who chooses a consequential question, method and depth. This happens after changed evidence and returned work reach the lead; it does not reopen waiting cases or start a parallel branch. The task and originating lead-run event commit atomically and survive restart. The same lead turn cannot produce duplicate continuation tasks. New work still passes the normal spending, storage, provider and cancellation controls, and every returned task requires lead interpretation. This replaces direction-wide idle with investigation, not repeated paid status requests or a strategy quota.

Discovery archives transport revisions separately from model wakes. For Hacker News search, changed returned stories wake research; request timing fields and estimated total-result counts alone do not. Both kinds of response retain their original bytes and provenance. Other API responses retain conservative change detection, and edits or reversions to returned story content remain new evidence.

`request_data` takes provider arguments separately from its freeform rationale. One call requests one dataset. Those same arguments are stored and passed to the acquisition process without parsing Markdown. Invalid requests report a concrete operational error; they do not create an approval requirement. A corrected request can explicitly supersede its queued predecessor while preserving the original record. The runtime holds credentials and verifies immutable snapshots. The existing watcher runs acquisition asynchronously alongside source retrieval, without occupying the delegated research slot or blocking the lead. Historical options acquisition retains successful pages and publishes completed expiry partitions. Partial coverage remains visibly incomplete until the original request finishes.

Optional forecasts and monitoring policies also use native tool arguments. Their reasoning has no required headings. Prospective commitments remain immutable and visible. Monitoring triggers independent review rather than orders; qualifying a new paper checkpoint still requires canonical diagnostics, independent evidence and declared monitoring. The active paper risk limits and operator-authorized spending ceiling remain in force.

Original sealed evidence for cited outcomes is mounted into delegated review workspaces. Successful commands are reproducibility evidence, not proof of a scientific interpretation. Agent criticism must examine original inputs, assumptions, arithmetic and alternative explanations.

Research RPC and canonical evaluations have no default study deadline. Quiet subprocesses are not killed by default. Operator cancellation remains available; `AR_TOOL_INACTIVITY_MS`, if explicitly configured, is an operational override. Numerical evaluation defaults to one process on the shared host. Environment inventory uses package metadata, and building a research prompt never launches dependency probes.

See [the incident postmortem](research-postmortem-2026-09-13.md) for the observed failures, corrections and verification limits. Earlier schema-14 coverage and framing prescriptions are historical and superseded.
