# Research goal: build a substantive UAV intelligence method

Continue the existing visual-model program, but first repair its experimental foundation. The primary use case is moving quadrotor navigation through cluttered, partially observed 3D scenes to sequential waypoints under inference delay. The first worker trained compact models in a small custom simulator; its code and weights are engineering scaffolds, not a verdict on the research idea. Its visualized flight barely translated and mostly commanded hover; ranking accuracy did not establish navigation. Do not spend another cycle enlarging that toy dataset or running another loss ablation.

Develop an actual research contribution for UAV navigation, tracking, or exploration. The original ideas and literature are staged under `.research-guidance/`. Consider learned visual judgment, action-conditioned prediction/world models, VLM/VLA, shared fast/slow computation, geometry and memory, but choose the problem and architecture yourself. Do not assume all modules belong in one system. Look for a concrete unsolved failure in primary papers and released implementations, then change the model, learning objective, data flow or system architecture in a way that plausibly addresses it. A renamed standard block, tiny heuristic, extra benchmark, or module collage is not enough.

The next implementation task is environment-first. Choose an established research environment, public real-scene reconstruction, or defensible real-to-sim asset based on the literature and available compute. Adapt it so the vehicle visibly moves through 3D clutter, camera observations are egocentric, obstacles can occlude route choices, collisions and braking matter, and inference age can be injected and measured. Implement the route/task adapter, data collection, visualization and route/collision/latency metrics. Do not train a new learned mechanism until this foundation is demonstrated. Then choose the model change from a specific residual failure and compare against a strong matched baseline. If compute or data limit the study, state exactly what was and was not tested; do not replace the use case with a low-dimensional proxy.

Keep a readable method account, runnable code, raw measurements, weights and a continuation note. Use `MODEL_IMPLEMENTATION.json` to carry their paths between tasks; it can live with the project code. Checkpoint reusable progress and record bounded intermediate results without repeating a receipt-style audit. Do not force novelty or performance claims from preliminary evidence; do not stop at a literature-only proposal when implementation is feasible.

Use the configured Spark research model and the existing 60% local VRAM guard. No physical flight or additional spending is authorized.
