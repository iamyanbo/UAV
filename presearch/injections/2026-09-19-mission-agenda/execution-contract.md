# Shared contract: mission first, mechanism second

User steering, September 19, 2026: navigation, tracking and spatial exploration are the main research branches. Start from mission objective -> important failure -> closest implemented methods -> residual hypothesis -> executable child experiment. These are three investigations under the existing `uav-navigation` direction, not a new scheduler or the runtime's formal program API. Keep ordinary continuous Spark orchestration. The cards propose hypotheses, not established novel contributions or completed experiments.

One first child per branch is ready for investigation. Later children depend on what is learned; there is no requirement to execute all children or combine all modules. Interpret one completed task before selecting its next useful child. Existing pending module ideas are merged as implementation options; never revive all five as independent priorities. Preserve completed evidence and any already running task. Administrative supersession is neither a failed experiment nor scientific rejection.

## Execution, limits and uncertainty

Normal preflight applies. Complete exploratory offline/simulation tasks can run with unresolved novelty and without an additional human approval loop. An exact equivalent supplies a baseline or invalidates the proposed novelty, not necessarily the usefulness of a bounded test. The lead should check close methods while building a feasible test; no source quota or exhaustive-search gate. If the running supervisor explicitly requires approval, supply its concrete missing review once. Physical flight remains unauthorized.

Keep the causal mechanism in the pilot. A perfect-state scalar benchmark is plumbing, not evidence of visual UAV capability. Use actual rendered partial observations, bounded sensing and actuator dynamics; advance physics during inference. For a reduced planar/constant-altitude pilot state that limitation, then test 3D feasibility before flight claims. A full VLM/VLA, learned world model, JEPA, JEV-like scorer or 3DGS is optional. Record actual deployed models and do not rename a small predictor a VLM or visual JEPA.

Share timestamped observations, a geometric local controller and an event-driven simulator when feasible. Measure capture-to-applied-command latency, p50/p95/p99, age of semantic/identity/map evidence, query rate, throughput, speed, braking/turn limits, collision, mission outcome and progress. The geometric reaction path may remain fast while semantics are slow. Charge computation and sensing travel to the mission; neither permanent hovering nor stopping the simulated clock is an improvement. Compare at matched useful progress/time and resource budgets, including a strong non-neural baseline.

CPU-first is allowed. On the 8 GiB RTX 3060 Ti, respect the user's approximately 4.8 GiB / 60% VRAM preference, check total observed memory including other software, and reduce resolution/batch/model or use CPU when necessary. A PyTorch per-process fraction <=0.6 is only an allocator limit, not a global cap. Never kill unrelated processes. Spark is the reasoning provider, not an allocated experimental GPU. No full foundation-model training is promised. If needed, train a compact head/controller on cached features; measure complete encoder-plus-head latency and memory, not the head alone.

## Evaluation and falsifiers

Keep training/calibration worlds separate from held-out topology, appearance and dynamics. No hidden map, future target trajectory, future frame, simulator truth or test label enters a deployed decision; such information is reserved for the evaluator or explicitly labelled training teacher/oracle upper bound. Equalize observations, action feasibility, compute and controller capacity except the proposed intervention. Include ablations that make decisions differ, adverse cases and uncertainty across independent environments. Label simplified implementations as such, not reproductions of named papers.

Distinguish invalid/non-discriminating pilots from negative hypotheses: if the labels are degenerate, the learner is privileged, or compared actions never differ, repair the experiment or record why it cannot answer the question. Reject the proposed residual if a stronger known baseline accounts for the benefit. Advance useful hypotheses to rendered closed-loop generalization, measured local inference and stronger baselines; a toy score is not a publication result.

Preserve `RESULT.md`, `metrics.json`, raw per-episode output, commands/exit codes, configuration/seeds/splits, code identity, deployed inputs, baseline implementations, ablations, timing/memory, representative failures, prior-art boundary and the next decision. Distinguish source-reported results, our measurements and proposed tests. Record novelty and empirical status separately. After a finite plan closes, ordinary Spark research continues, organized by these mission branches rather than a repeated module list.
