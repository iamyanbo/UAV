# Architecture-level prior-art audit for the invention reboot

Operator review, September 19, 2026. This is a bounded primary-source audit, not a certificate that every paper or implementation has been found. New proposals remain **novelty unresolved; operators implemented; full methods untrained**. Paper-reported performance is not our reproduced performance. A paper's benchmark limitation does not establish that its authors' method cannot work elsewhere.

## 1. Late semantic corrections: closest implementations

- [CloudEdgeVLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00569v1), method inspected: a cloud OpenVLA-OFT representation is fused with fresh local SigLIP features by a local action model. Training pairs current and delayed semantic features with the current action target. This already covers latency-tolerant cloud/edge fusion. Its fusion construction is different from training a correction operator against chronological recurrent replay, but this inspection does not establish absence of every equivalent in its code or related work. Compare a history-aware, capacity-matched fusion implementation, not only overwrite or drop-stale baselines. Report the cost of preparing and applying the correction.
- [SPARK-VLN](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.16806v1), method inspected: a slow reasoner streams intermediate hidden states; sequence-to-slot compression supplies a fast flow-matching planner with current RGB-D. A geometric critic selects trajectories. Thus streaming slow semantics into fast navigation and evaluating movement during inference are established. The proposal instead asks how an interpretation of a past state changes a present recurrent state after intervening evidence. A shared/faster backbone alone is not a residual contribution. This paper's SPARK name is unrelated to our DGX Spark reasoning server.
- [ReMem-VLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12942v1), architecture and memory updates inspected: a frozen Qwen3-VL backbone supplies representations, recurrent memory queries are propagated with an EMA-style update, and a connector/diffusion action path is trained with a past-observation reconstruction objective. Persistent memory and multirate models are established. The proposed method needs to show a distinct benefit from explicit timestamped correction and executed-history replay supervision.
- [Out-of-sequence measurements in robot localization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3376572/), delayed-measurement treatment inspected: restoring a past state and reprocessing intervening measurements is classical. **Replay itself is not our invention.** The speculative residual is an amortized neural semantic correction operator, trained to preserve both replay state and decisions under later contradictory observations. Neural smoothing, delayed belief updates and history-conditioned fusion remain essential citation chains to investigate.

The method must beat a strong history-aware fusion model or offer a measured cost/accuracy advantage over exact replay. A time-conditioned residual MLP with no distinctive training or demonstrated information handling is insufficient. The fixed-size summary adds work every frame; count that work, not only reply-time latency.

## 2. Observation-branch predictive representation

- [Branch-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05238v3), main method and supplementary finite-support construction inspected: predicts multiple future latent atoms with a router, trains distributional predictions using an energy-score objective and compares alternative objectives/control capacity. Multiple latent futures and avoiding their collapse into a mean are already implemented. The proposed difference is a likelihood channel conditional on a retained world hypothesis and candidate sensing action, coupling predictive observation branches to posterior updates used by a planner. It must beat an ordinary learned Bayesian filter, not just deterministic JEPA.
- [UWM-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25313v1), representation and dynamics inspected: a density-matrix latent represents uncertainty, with action-dependent unitary evolution designed to preserve relevant uncertainty structure during prediction. This rules out broad claims that JEPA cannot represent uncertainty or that uncertainty-preserving prediction is new. Its initial settings do not establish visual UAV transfer; nor do they disprove it. Our proposed finite hypothesis/observation construction has different assumptions and significant hypothesis-generation costs.
- [ARC-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05461v1), reported findings/limitations inspected: closed-loop replanning can conceal poor action ranking in evaluated latent world models. This motivates checking the decision induced by a representation; it is not evidence that every JEPA fails or that a new ranking head is novel.
- [TD-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25337v1) and [AC-MTM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17542), initial primary-source leads, not full code audits: temporal-cost prediction and contrastive inverse-action learning already occupy nearby objective-design space. Retrieve implementation details before claiming either addition as an invention.
- [Observable quotient world models](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950705126014942), indexed publisher lead: potentially very close work on partial observability, predictive abstraction and information acquisition. The displayed issue date is October 9, 2026, later than this audit. Online-first availability/date and full method were not independently established here. Treat it as an unresolved concurrent-work lead, not a verified already-published implementation or something safely ignored.

Bayes' rule, belief-space planning, expected information gain and predictive-state representations are established. In particular, the identity `sum_k p(k) p(h|k) = p(h)` is elementary marginalization, not a novel theorem. The contribution hypothesis concerns a learned visual/geometric representation and its observation-to-belief interface. It can fail if a conventional learned Bayes filter has the same mechanism or if a learned hypothesis bank does not preserve ambiguity. Multiple plausible renders do not become measurements by being imagined.

## 3. Capture-constrained environment-policy co-training

- [ReMiDi, ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/beukman24a.html), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.12284v1) inspected: refined minimax-regret environment design addresses irreducible regret under partial observability. Sequential regret buffers and indistinguishable-trajectory handling are already part of this field. **Removing impossible, oracle-only challenges is not by itself new.** A capture-constrained ReMiDi-style method is a necessary competitor; it could account for the entire apparent improvement.
- [Generating Transferable Adversarial Simulation Scenarios for Self-Driving via Neural Rendering, CoRL 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/abeysirigoonawardena23a.html), proceedings abstract inspected: learned scene rendering is already used to generate adversarial scenarios. Full optimizer, constraints and code still need inspection before a precise residual can be supported. “Train on generated difficult scenes” is too broad.
- [Vid2Sim](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.06693v1), architecture reviewed in the existing full-stack audit: video-based reconstruction/system identification already supports training and transfer. [EmbodiedSplat, ICCV 2025](https://www.openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Chhablani_EmbodiedSplat_Personalized_Real-to-Sim-to-Real_Navigation_with_Gaussian_Splats_from_a_Mobile_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf), proceedings lead: personalized Gaussian real-to-sim-to-real navigation is established. A newly captured site or Gaussian renderer is not enough.
- PAIRED, prioritized level replay and active perception are older enabling methods. Follow their citations alongside ReMiDi rather than treating UAV terminology as the novelty boundary. [World Action Verifier](https://world-action-verifier.github.io/), official project lead, also makes action-reachability verification of generated futures relevant prior art.

The tentative residual is joint optimization of capture-consistent hidden completions and an observation-limited sensing policy, with paired histories that remain behaviorally equivalent until a reachable reveal. It needs a real alternating generator/learner, preserved capture views, nontrivial hidden geometry, and held-out worlds outside the generator. Pinning observed scene parameters does not guarantee unchanged rendered images: illumination, occlusion and shadows still require checks. Irreducible aliasing cannot be fixed by supplying a privileged teacher at evaluation.

## Original ideas retained, with narrower claims

- Fast local visual decision model: NanoJev/typed heads and compact VLMs are implementation foundations. Method 1 changes what the slow result means and how it updates the fast state. A Qwen swap is optional, not the contribution.
- Shared slow/fast model: [ROCKET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17951), [official implementation overview](https://github.com/CASE-Lab-UMD/ROCKET-VLA/blob/main/README.md), uses nested/Matryoshka-related alignment representations. This narrows broad claims; it does not prove the user's particular shared recurrent slow/fast controller is already implemented. Start with the correction mechanism, then test shared-prefix deployment as an extension.
- Geometric JEPA/native Gaussian representation: Method 2 keeps the predictive geometric ambition. Direct Gaussian tokens are a later representation choice, not implemented by the starter tensors. SkyJEPA's state inputs must not be mistaken for a trained visual backbone.
- Rapid real-to-sim/world atlas: Method 3 makes reconstruction part of an adaptive training algorithm. It does not assume a single photorealistic reconstruction is ground truth in unobserved areas. Real-transfer claims require actual captures and physical/dynamics validation.

## Search boundary and remaining work

This pass used the original reports, architecture supplements, stored CURI investigations/source titles, and primary-source searches across delayed/cloud-edge VLA, streamed VLN, recurrent memory, out-of-sequence filtering, probabilistic/branch JEPA, partial-observability world models, adversarial environment design, neural rendering and capture-to-simulation navigation. Main methods were inspected where noted. No external baseline was reproduced during this audit, and not every repository, supplement, thesis or forward citation was read. The indexed dates of recent preprints/publisher leads must be checked against their actual version history. Earlier confident “unclaimed combination” wording is withdrawn.

For each method, the worker should inspect the closest missing implementation details and write a source-to-algorithm comparison before strong novelty claims. This is ongoing research, not a queue-blocking demand to finish all literature. If a closer method is found, revise the proposed information flow or learning mechanism, or mark it a reproduction and develop another candidate. Component overlap permits a nontrivial improvement; absence of an exact title does not prove one.
