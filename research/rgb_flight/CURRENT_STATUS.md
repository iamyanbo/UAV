## Strategy correction

Continuing world and policy updates was the wrong next priority for achieving metric handover. All 11 r72 development fits that reached the similarity-fit stage failed residual/uncertainty checks. On train-00093, selected because it had the most supported map publications, actual recorded-data diagnosis found 69 paired tracking frames, relative-rotation RMSE 0.84 degrees and post-hoc similarity-aligned translation RMSE 0.266 m over a 3.02 m motion radius. That supports investigating metric VO and alignment first; it does not qualify SLAM globally or supply runtime scale. Privileged labels and the fitted scale were used only for this offline diagnosis.
